Model correction

After a flight, Liftoff fits the drag coefficient to the measured altitude by least squares.

The physics

Drag grows with the square of speed:

D = ½ · ρ · v² · Cd · A

ρ is air density, A the frontal area, and Cd the drag coefficient: the one number that captures the rocket’s shape, fins and surface. It is the hardest to know in advance, so Liftoff measures it.

AI cost this session

Physics never goes to the AI. Common requests are handled by rules with zero tokens, repeats come from cache, and context is compressed with The Token Company’s bear-2 before each call. Baseline = a large model sent the full context on every request.
