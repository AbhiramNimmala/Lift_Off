The physics
Drag grows with the square of speed:
D = ½ · ρ · v² · Cd · A
Cd captures the rocket’s shape, fins and surface. It is the hardest number to know in advance, so Liftoff measures it from every flight.
Stability
The rocket is stable when the centre of pressure (orange ring) sits behind the centre of gravity (white ring). Aim for 1–2.5 body diameters.
